Olitun CMS Architecture

App Content Hierarchy & Flow

Understand how standard and custom categories nest subcategories, lessons, and content blocks to build an interactive, immersive learning experience on mobile.

1

Category

High-level modules (Standard sections like Alphabets/Vocabulary or Custom pages created by Admins).

2

Subcategory

Specific sub-groups (e.g. "Greetings", "Animal Vocabulary") inside a Category that group related lessons.

3

Lesson

Individual lesson page (e.g. "Lesson Vocab Basics") containing titles, levels, and content blocks.

4

Content / Blocks

Granular building blocks inside the lesson detail slider (Images, Lotties, Audio, and rich Text Blocks).
