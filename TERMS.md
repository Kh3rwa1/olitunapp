# Olitun Terms Of Use

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Olitun is provided as an educational app for learning Ol Chiki and related Santali learning content. By using the app, you agree to use it respectfully and only for lawful learning, teaching, and administration.

## Accounts

Users are responsible for the accounts they create and for keeping sign-in access secure. Admin access is limited to authorized team members and must only be used to manage appropriate learning content. Users may delete their accounts at any time from Profile settings.

## Content & AI Processing

Learning content, media, translations, and examples should not be copied, uploaded, or shared unless you have the rights to use them:
- **AI Studio & Voice Synthesis:** When using AI Studio (document scan, voice transcription, AI translation) or Santali AI Voice synthesis, you must only submit content you own or have explicit permission to process.
- **Shared Impersonal Cache:** To minimize compute overhead and maintain fast, affordable access, synthesized audio clips and translation pairs are indexed by impersonal cryptographic hashes and cached across users for 90 days. Cached audio and translation entries contain no personal identifiers and are not per-user deletable.
- Admins must verify copyright and cultural appropriateness before publishing media or lesson content.

## Availability & Local Storage

Features may change as the app improves:
- **Offline Operation:** The app functions offline using local storage in the device's operating-system-protected application sandbox (unencrypted at rest by default). Users are responsible for maintaining device-level screen locks and physical device security.
- **Cloud Features:** Cloud sync, account features, external media streaming, AI Studio, and voice synthesis require network access.

## Prohibited Use

Do not abuse the app, bypass access controls, attempt unauthorized function RPCs, automate scraping or synthetic load against translation or voice synthesis backends, upload unsafe or infringing content, or use the platform in a way that harms other users, the cultural community, or the service infrastructure.
